About Me

I am exploring life and finishing small projects that fascinate me one by one.

I am an undergraduate who loves theories! I have studied a lot of academic theories, such as quantum mechanics and general relativity. Although understanding them and finding out that they actually work is exciting I occasionally feel unsettled and want to make something out of what I have learned or any of my ideas. Thus, I am now trying to "get my hands dirty" and make whatever comes to my mind rather than thinking too much to theorize. This website might showcase some projects that I feel are the most meaningful, and maybe some of my own philosophies or theories.
